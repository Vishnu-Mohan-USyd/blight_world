## Scope and exclusions

This validation covers persistent sliding scrape synthesis, contact hysteresis/release, positioned full-room binaural rendering, and independent clip/continuous capacity. Rolling, clothing, and air sounds are not implemented. The constrained fixtures are engineering experiments and do not demonstrate gait, manipulation skill, or an agent policy.
