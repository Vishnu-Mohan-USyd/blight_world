# Recorded barefoot concrete footsteps

`48k/footstep_concrete_0.wav` through `48k/footstep_concrete_3.wav` are
individual impacts from **concrete_01c_barefeet_run.wav** by **sturmankin**,
Freesound sound 272287. The author describes real barefoot running on
concrete, recorded with a ZOOM H2 in a house basement and normalized in
Audacity. This is an indoor recording, not an outdoor street recording.

Source: https://freesound.org/people/sturmankin/sounds/272287/

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, as stated on the source page:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The publicly provided high-quality preview was used:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/272/272287_4181170-hq.mp3
It is mono, 48 kHz MP3 at approximately 191 kbps. The crops were decoded to
mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV. They retain the preview's gain and pitch, with
0.5 ms fade-ins and 10 ms fade-outs at the cut edges. No equalization,
synthesis, pitch shifting, or additional reverberation was applied.

These are recorded footstep timbre proxies, not measured MIMo acoustics;
recording amplitudes are uncalibrated. Waveforms and format were inspected
to isolate attacks before neighboring foot movements. Direct listening was
unavailable in the asset-preparation tool session.
